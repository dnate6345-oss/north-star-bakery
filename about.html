About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a small neighborhood bake shop with one oven, a handful of family recipes, and a simple idea: a bakery should feel like part of the neighborhood. Over the years, commuters have made us part of their morning routine, families have celebrated birthdays with our cakes, and local event planners have trusted us with their biggest days.

We still bake the way we did on our first day — in small batches, by hand, and fresh every morning. When you walk in, you will smell bread in the oven, see the day's pastries in the case, and be greeted by people who are glad you came.

How We Source Our Ingredients

Good bread starts with good ingredients. We buy flour, eggs, dairy, honey, and seasonal fruit from local and regional farms and suppliers whenever we can, and we choose ingredients we would serve to our own families.

  • Flour and grains from regional mills
  • Local honey and seasonal fruit for our featured bakes
  • Real butter, eggs, and dairy with no shortcuts
  • Day-old bread shared with neighborhood food programs instead of wasted

Meet Our Team

Maria, Head Baker

Maria leads our early-morning bake and developed our Signature Loaf recipe. She believes every loaf should have a crisp crust, an open crumb, and a story worth sharing.

James, Pastry Baker

James makes our croissants, danish, and seasonal pastries. His cinnamon rolls are a weekend favorite with neighborhood families.

Priya, Cake Decorator and Counter Lead
